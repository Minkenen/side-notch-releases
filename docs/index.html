Side Notch

Everything you glance at, one edge away.

Side Notch is a thin line at the left edge of your screen. Point at it and a panel slides out with your music, calendar, weather, notes, clipboard and more. Move away and it's gone.

Download for Mac Apple silicon & Intel Also for Windows Free · updates itself

See it

The panel opens from the line and closes when you move away. Arrange the boxes how you like, in one or more columns.

The boxes

Pick the ones you want. Each one opens wider with its handle when you need more.

Install once. It keeps itself up to date.

When a new version is out, Side Notch downloads it in the background. A red dot appears on the settings button, and one click restarts into the new version. You never need to come back here.

Mac

  1. Open Side-Notch-mac.dmg and drag Side Notch to Applications.
  2. Open it. Point at the left edge of your screen, or press ⌥ Space.
  3. Some boxes ask for permission the first time, like Messages (Accessibility) or Photos. You choose.

Windows

  1. Run Side-Notch-Setup.exe. It installs for you only, no admin needed.
  2. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Point at the left edge of your screen, or press Ctrl Alt N.

On Windows

Side Notch runs on Windows 10 and 11. A few things lean on macOS and aren't there yet:

  • Messages: reading new messages from notifications is Mac only.
  • Photos: the automatic Photos library is Mac only. On Windows you add pictures or folders yourself.
  • System: GPU load and power use are shown on Mac only.
  • Batteries: Bluetooth devices like headphones and mice are shown on Mac only.
  • Shelf: AirDrop and the share menu are Mac only. Files can still be dragged out and zipped.
